# References for the Stommel/Boyd datasets

Full citations for `data/datasets/stommel_boyd2015.csv`, `stommel_boyd2015_volumes.csv`,
and `models.csv`. The short keys in each CSV's `Reference(s)` column resolve here.

Source figure: BioGeoSCAPES Science Plan, Figure 3.4 — time and space scales of
biological (green), physical (blue), and coupled physico-chemical/biological
(lime green) processes, overlaid with models 1–9. The background schematic is
adapted, with substantial modification by P. Boyd, from Figure 1 of Boyd et al.
(2016), © 2016 John Wiley & Sons Ltd.

> Note: the dataset filenames retain the historical `boyd2015` stem. The correct
> citation for the background schematic is Boyd et al. (2016); see issue for the
> filename rename.

## Open verification items

- **Number placement in the process CSVs**: `stommel_boyd2015.csv` /
  `_volumes.csv` Notes tag "Number 5 in diagram" on Habitat-scale hydrodynamics
  and "Number 7" on Mesoscale eddies. Per the caption, 5 = micro-scale
  microfluidics (Son et al. 2016) and 6 = high-resolution physical models
  (Mahadevan et al. 2012). A micro-scale chemotaxis model placed in a
  habitat-scale (100 m–10 km) box is a scale mismatch — either the figure
  places the marker there anyway, or the CSV note mis-transcribes 5 vs 6.
  Verify against the rendered figure before attaching model refs to process
  rows.
- **Moore et al. (2011)**: full citation unconfirmed (see model 8 above).
